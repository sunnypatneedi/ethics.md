This standalone draft generalizes Sunny's earlier product guidance:

- **Ace:** `ETHICS.md` and `SOUL.md`, added together September 29, 2026, in commit `b8893342ade374526964a8acf1b71a5ce6bbaa67`. The themes include consent, evidence, inference restraint, user-controlled actions, honest status, accessibility, and executable controls. Product implementation names and internal verification tables are excluded here.
- **Piki / People Wiki:** ethics and safety rails included in the local public-release staging commit `8205c7ec602e29c314d93a9971673819d2841d9b`, September 23, 2026. That staging tree's MIT notice credits Sunny Patneedi. The themes include relevant public sources, documented versus inferred claims, respectful research, private interpretive records, and draft-only outreach. This standalone policy permits separately authorized sending; Piki's own draft-only boundary remains specific to Piki.
- **OpenClaw:** its [SOUL template](https://docs.openclaw.ai/reference/templates/SOUL) inspired the idea of a visible Markdown persona/policy file. This release does not reproduce the SOUL template, ship OpenClaw code, or imply affiliation, endorsement, or an automatically loaded OpenClaw `ETHICS.md` convention. See [official workspace documentation](https://docs.openclaw.ai/concepts/agent-workspace) for its supported files.
